SeaFlow Library · Development history

Completed product generations are archived by final capability. This page does not reproduce individual development runs or audit records.

V0 | Foundation generation

Covered versions: V0.1–V0.9

Capabilities after completion

V0 established the legal-source foundation and controlled governance framework of SeaFlow Library. The system can receive, lock and verify legal text, create stable provision and citation units, perform deterministic retrieval over collections including Chinese maritime law, the Hague–Visby Rules and UNCLOS, and check source registration, file hashes and citation consistency. It also established project control, owner decision boundaries, the ShanHai librarian identity and generic ingestion capabilities for candidate source packages.

Capability boundaries

V0 delivered reliable legal-source infrastructure and controlled candidate workflows, rather than open production writes. Agent requests, source proposals and ShanHai identity status do not authorize canonical writes. Candidate ingestion is not formal source promotion; production execution and formal collection writes require separate owner approval. Unified SeaFlow interfaces, cross-machine installation, host-system integration and complete dual-platform handoff were not yet delivered in this generation.

What V1 takes forward

V1 builds on V0 to make the library installable, transferable and callable by SeaFlow. V1.0 establishes a portable bundle, Citation Packet, source routing, a JSONL bridge and Windows/Linux qualification. V1.1 addresses host-system testing feedback and redesigns current development, history navigation, the README and product contract. Later work covers controlled human and agent source-admission paths, formal release and production governance.
